  1. 0:00–0:40
    The waiting problem

    Guests wait to order or settle up. Groundwork gives them control from their phone while staff keep visibility.

  2. 0:40–2:00
    Place an order

    Add a burger with double patty and two lemonades. Add “No onions” and a 12% tip. The total is R369.60. Place the demo order, then refresh: the confirmation remains.

  3. 2:00–3:15
    Show the restaurant

    Open the restaurant ticket. Move it from Received to Preparing to Ready. The guest sees the same status. Connected mode saves this in the database.

  4. 3:15–4:00
    Explain the business

    R349 per venue per month is a pricing hypothesis for QR pay. Ordering is an expansion opportunity. Partner costs and margins still need validation.

  5. 4:00–5:00
    Ask for a controlled pilot

    Back one till integration, one payment partner and two venues. Agree the budget and success thresholds before launch. Measure adoption, payment time and staff intervention.
